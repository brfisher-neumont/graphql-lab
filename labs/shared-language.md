# Shared Language

A glossary of the business terms used throughout the FocusFlow requirements. Use these terms consistently across capabilities, epics, and stories so everyone — product, design, and stakeholders — means the same thing when they say them.

## Summary

At its heart, FocusFlow brings a user into a **Focus Session** set within a chosen **Theme** and paired with a **Playlist**, giving their work a distraction-free, immersive backdrop. Staying with it day after day builds a **Streak**, a visible sign of consistency that keeps them coming back. Beyond individual effort, a user can lean on an **Accountability Group**, where peers see and celebrate one another's progress, turning personal focus into a shared, motivating habit.

## Terms

**Accountability Group (Network of Responsibility)**
A group of peers who support one another in staying committed to their focus goals by seeing each other's activity and celebrating progress together.

**Activity Feed**
The shared stream within a group where members see updates such as completed goals and congratulatory reactions.

**Ambient Sound**
A background sound — such as rain, café, fireplace, ocean, forest, or thunder — used to set the mood for a focus session.

**Business Owner/Admin**
The role responsible for getting FocusFlow ready and available to visitors, including the starting content they encounter.

**Congratulatory Reaction**
A response a group member sends to celebrate a peer's completed goal or session.

**Dashboard**
The personalized home screen a user reaches once onboarding is complete, giving them an at-a-glance view of their streak and starting point for a new session.

**Default Playlist**
The ready-made set of songs and sounds available to every visitor from day one, before they've made any choice of their own.

**Default Theme**
The ready-made visual and ambient environment shown to visitors and new users before they've chosen their own.

**Designer**
The role responsible for creating the themes and playlists that shape the FocusFlow experience.

**Focus Session (Session)**
A dedicated period of focused work that brings together a timer, a task, and a chosen theme and audio environment.

**Goal**
Something a user is working toward that can be marked complete, prompting recognition from their accountability group.

**Group Invite Code**
A code a user enters to join an accountability group.

**Group Member**
A user who belongs to an accountability group and can see their peers' progress.

**Landing Page**
The first page a visitor sees when they arrive at FocusFlow, before signing up for an account.

**New User**
Someone who has just created an account and is being guided through onboarding.

**Objective**
The stated purpose of a focus session — for example, Coding, Homework, Reading, Writing, Design, Research, or Custom.

**Onboarding**
The guided introduction a new user goes through, learning FocusFlow's key offerings and setting up their first theme, playlist, task, and session.

**Playlist**
A curated collection of songs or sounds a user can choose to play during a session.

**Project**
A grouping that organizes related tasks together.

**Session Duration**
The length of time chosen for a focus session — 25, 45, 60, or 90 minutes, or a custom length.

**Streak**
The number of consecutive days a user has completed at least one focus session.

**Longest Streak**
A user's best streak achieved to date, which stays in place even after their current streak resets.

**Task**
An individual work item a user tracks, which can be tied to a project and brought into a focus session.

**Theme**
A paired visual style and ambient sound set that sets the mood for a session.

**User**
A person using FocusFlow to plan, focus on, and complete their work.

**Visitor**
Someone browsing FocusFlow without having created an account.

**Volume**
The adjustable loudness of a playlist or ambient sound, controllable while previewing or during an active session.
